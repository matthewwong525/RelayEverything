title: Location

Relay uses your location two ways: it pins each typed note to where you are, and it keeps a history of where you went, so ring notes get a place too.

## Pin notes to a place

**Pin notes to a place** is on when you connect. The app asks for your location once, right after the phone connects. Each typed note then keeps where you were. The phone names the place itself, such as a street.

## Location history

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Turn on **Location history**.
3. Allow your location. An iPhone asks a second time: choose **Always Allow**, so the app can record when it is closed.

The app keeps a point each 100 m you move, not a constant fix, so it uses little battery. On Android, the notification *Relay is keeping your places* stays while it records: Android asks for this.

A ring note takes the history point nearest in time, up to 10 minutes before or after you spoke.

## Turn it off

Turn off **Pin notes to a place** or **Location history** in **Settings**. The phone stops at once. [Privacy](../../privacy/) says what the server keeps.
