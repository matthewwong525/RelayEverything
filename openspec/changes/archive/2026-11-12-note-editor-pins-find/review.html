Why

On 2026-11-12 you typed in a long note on your iPhone and compared it with Apple Notes. Three things felt wrong. The line you type goes under the floating tool bar, so you cannot see the cursor. The tool bar floats about 20 points over the keyboard, where Apple's sits closer. And there is no Undo button while you type.

You also want two Apple Notes features: a Pinned part at the top of the notes list that folds, and Pin Note and Find in Note from a note's ⋯ menu.

What Changes

  1. 1
    The line you type stays in view. When you tap into a note or type at its end, the page scrolls so the cursor stays above the tool bar, not under it. The tool bar moves down to about 6 points over the keyboard, as Apple's does. No real picture of the keyboard: only an iPhone draws it, so a grey block stands in. The device check takes the real picture at /verify.
  2. 2
    Undo shows while you type. A round Undo button shows at the top, left of share and ⋯, after your first change. One tap takes back the last change. After an Undo, a Redo button shows beside it. Undo goes back only to when you opened the note. The keyboard stays up.
  3. 3
    Pinned notes stay at the top of the notes list. A Pinned part shows above Today when a note is pinned. A tap on Pinned folds it to one line, and a second tap opens it. The app keeps the fold after it closes. A pinned note shows only in Pinned, not also under its day. Search and the filters (All, Voice, Photos and files) apply to pinned notes too. A swipe right on a row pins or unpins it, as in Apple Notes.
  4. 4
    Pin Note and Find in Note are in a note's ⋯ menu. Pin Note pins the note, and then reads Unpin Note. Find in Note puts a find bar over the keyboard: the words to find, 2 of 14, and up and down arrows. The arrows go to each match in turn and select it, and capital letters do not matter. ✓ closes the find bar. Finding never changes the note.
  5. 5
    A pin follows you everywhere. A note pinned on one phone shows in Pinned on your other phones and on your Relay Everything page, and a pin on the page shows on your phones. When two pin changes meet, the newer one wins. A pin is not a change to the note's words, so your webhooks get no message for it. Signed out, or with only your own webhook, the pin stays on the phone.
  6. 6
    The guide says it. The Your notes page on the website tells how to pin, fold Pinned, undo and find in a note.

Non-goals: no Lock Note, Move, Duplicate or Scan. No long-press menu on a row. No Find in Note on the web page, where the browser has its own find. No change to how notes send.

What the build needs

  1. Nothing. The real keyboard and the swipe show only on an iPhone: the device check takes them at /verify, and you try them on the next TestFlight build.

Decisions

  1. asked2026-11-12

    Asked whether a pin shows on the other phones and the web page or only on the phone that pinned it → chose everywhere, through the server.

  2. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pin sends no webhook message, because it changes how the list looks, not the note's words.

  3. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: Undo shows after the first change and Redo only after an Undo, both at the top left of share, because Apple Notes on iOS 26 puts Undo there.

  4. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: Find in Note selects the current match and does not color every match, because a color on the words would be saved into the note.

  5. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a swipe right on a row also pins, because Apple Notes does it and it costs little; no long-press menu.

  6. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: Find in Note is phone-only; the web page keeps the browser's own find (Ctrl+F).

  7. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the fold of Pinned stays on each phone, because it is how one screen looks.

  8. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the tool bar sits 6 points over the keyboard, measured from your Apple Notes picture.

  9. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the server test is pin-note.test.ts, not notes-pin.test.ts, to match edit-note and delete-note.

  10. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the fold of Pinned reuses the folded headers the phone already keeps (setFolded('Pinned'), in memory on the web), not a new pinnedFolded setting, because a second store for folds would duplicate it.

  11. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pull drops a waiting pin older than the server's, in place of "apply only when no note.pin waits", because that pin would get 409 after the pull and leave the phone on the older pin.

  12. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pin made while sending was off goes when sending turns on to Relay Everything, tracked by a pin_unsent mark, because a pull reads only notes changed on the server; a pull still queues such a pin when it brings that note and the pin is newer.

  13. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: once the server takes a pin, the phone keeps the server's pinChangedAt from the PUT notes/pin answer, and a pull of a note with no unsent pin takes the server's pin and time, so a phone clock set ahead never wins over a later pin made elsewhere.

  14. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pin made on the phone is stamped at least 1 ms after the note's last known pin change, so a phone clock that runs behind never makes it look older; a pin refused as older (409) clears its pin_unsent mark and the next pull takes the server's pin, not a 409 answer that carries the server's pin, because the action contract sends only an error's code and message.

  15. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pin that matches the server's still saves its newer time (with no webhook message), so an older change made elsewhere still loses.

  16. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a pin refused with 403 asks GET key, as photos do: a key that answers means a server older than pins, and the pin is dropped so the other uploads still go.

  17. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a tap in the words while finding ends find mode, as in Apple Notes, not a recount of the matches.

  18. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: a webhook ignores note.pin: the outbox counts it as sent with no request.

  19. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the page's bottom padding stays 120, because it is already more than the 64-point bar margin.

  20. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the caret stays above the bar by the design's fallback, a post-frame scroll to the caret on each move, not scrollBottomInset, because flutter_quill 11.6 adds that inset over the caret's top edge too, so a caret moving up went under the top edge.

  21. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: Find in Note shows only once a voice note has its words, and Pin Note only on a note the phone kept.

  22. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the pinned notes sort newest first by the note's time, as the day groups do, not by when they were pinned.

  23. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the phone's pin tests sit in store_test.dart and note_sync_test.dart (its app group runs the controller), not controller_test.dart and data_test.dart.

  24. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the step's schema test is its own file, note-pins-schema.test.ts, as note-places-schema.test.ts is, because space-schema.test.ts would pass the 500-line limit.

  25. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the server tests ran before all phone source was written, against the build order, because the server part was finished first.

  26. log2026-11-12

    Built and saved: every task but the iPhone device check is done and checked; two review rounds found 8 problems, all fixed with tests (an older server's 403 on a pin, a pin made while sending was off, find after a tap, the caret moving up, phone clocks ahead or behind, a matching pin's time).

  27. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the Pinned heading (and Home's folding names) gets a screen-reader node of its own, because the iPhone check showed its box covering the note card, so a tap on Pinned opened the note; fixed in the app with a test, not by moving the flow's tap.

  28. assumed2026-11-12

    Assumed: the fixed tour's settings step gets 40 seconds, not 20, to scroll to Save, because the second iPhone check failed there twice on a slow Mac with Save on the screen (each screen read took about 10 seconds); the app's Settings did not change.

  29. assumed2026-11-12
