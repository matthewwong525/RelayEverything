The Relay Everything app opens on **Home**, a screen of folders. Your notes are the first folder. The app shows the notes that you type or say into this phone, and keeps them on the phone. Once you sign in to Relay Everything, it also shows the notes from your other phones, email, Claude Code, Codex and your AI apps: [Your notes on every phone](#your-notes-on-every-phone).

## Home

Home has a large **Home** title, the [sync state](#the-sync-state) under it only when notes wait or can not go, and the round [gear button](#the-gear-and-your-relay-everything-page) at the top right. Then three cards of folders. Each row shows a name, its status or its count, and a chevron. Tap a row to open it.

| Card | Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Your notes | **Notes** | Every note on this phone, with [filters](#filter-your-notes) for voice notes and for photos and files. |
| **On this phone** | **Location** | Location history: *Off*, or its mode and when the points last went, such as *Save battery · Sent 14:05*. [Location](../location/) |
| | **Health** | *Off*, or when the readings last went and how many wait. [Health](../health/) |
| | **Calendar** | *Off*, or how many calendars and when the events last went. [Calendar](../calendar/) |
| | **Photos** | *Off*, or *On* and if photos also go on mobile data. [Photos](../photos/) |
| **Other connections** | **Connect your AI** | *Let your AI read your notes*. Your computers with Relay Everything in Claude Code or Codex, and your **Saved chats**, then the AI connector's address and the AI apps you connected. [Connect your AI](../../connections/connect-your-ai/) |
| | **Email notes** | *Email a note to your own address*. Your two notes addresses, each with **Copy**. [Email notes](../email/) |

Tap **On this phone** or **Other connections** to fold that card away. Tap it again to open it. The phone remembers which cards you folded.

**Search** and **New note** stay at the bottom of Home, so you still write or talk with one tap.

Home holds no setup. Your account, sign-in, phones and webhooks are in [Settings](../settings/#account), on its account card.

## Read your notes

Tap **Notes** on Home. The list shows your notes, newest first, under the **Notes** title. Tap **Home** at the top left to go back. The notes are in groups by day: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Previous 7 days**, **Previous 30 days**, then one group for each month. Each group is one card, with one row for each note:

- The note's first line, in bold, without its style marks. A voice note with no words of yours shows the start of its transcript, or **Writing the words…** until the phone writes it. [Voice notes](../voice-notes/)
- The time, then the rest of the words. Today and yesterday show the time, this week the day, and older notes the date. A note with a recording also shows a microphone and its first recording's length after the time, such as *0:42*. A note from somewhere else also shows where it came from, such as *8:02 · Email*.
- **Waiting to send**, only on a note that waits. A sent note says nothing about sending.
- The first picture, small, at the right. [Add photos and files](#add-photos-and-files)

A row has no tag: a typed note and a voice note look the same, except for the microphone. A row shows no place, because a note keeps no place: with **Location history** on, where you made it is a point in your [location history](../location/#where-you-made-a-note).

Your [pinned notes](#pin-a-note) show first, in their own **Pinned** card above the day groups.

Before your first note, the list says **No notes yet**: *Tap New note to write one, or hold it to talk.* With sending off, it also says *Notes stay on this phone until you sign in, in Settings.*

When you scroll the list up, the large title goes, and one row stays at the top: the small **Notes** title with the count under it, such as *12 notes*. While notes wait, the state shows there in place of the count, such as *3 waiting*.

## Filter your notes

Under the title, three filters show how many notes each holds: **All**, **Voice** and **Photos and files**. Tap one to see only its notes. **All** is first.

| Filter | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every note. |
| **Voice** | Only the notes with a recording. An empty one says *Hold New note to talk.* [Voice notes](../voice-notes/) |
| **Photos and files** | The notes with a photo or a file. A link alone does not put a note here. An empty one says **No photos or files yet**: *A note with a photo or a file shows here.* |

A note stays in its place in **All** when you add a photo to it. The list keeps the filter you picked while you open a note and come back. [Search](#search-your-notes) finds only the notes of the filter you picked.

## Open and change a note

Tap a note's row to open it on its own page:

- The date and the time.
- All of its words, in their styles.
- Each picture, full width, at its place in the words. Tap a picture to open it.
- Each other file, as a row with its name and size, at its place in the words. Tap it to open it.
- On a voice note, the recording's card under the date, before the words: the player, and a **Transcript** row. Tap it to open the recording's page, with all of its words. A recording you add later shows at its place in the words, as a picture does. [Play a note back](../voice-notes/#play-a-note-back)

A note from before files went in the words keeps its look: the recording it started with shows first, and its other files after the words.

The page shows the date alone at the top. It shows no tag and nothing about sending: a note that waits sends by itself.

A web address in the words, such as `https://example.com` or `www.example.com`, shows in green and underlined. Tap it to open it in the phone's browser. This works for an address that you typed or pasted as plain text too. While you type, a tap on a link puts the cursor there, as in Apple Notes. The note's words do not change: the link shows only on the phone.

To put a link on words, such as *the menu*, select them and tap **Link**. A box asks for the address. If you copied a web address, the box shows it already. Tap **Done**. The words show as a link, and the note keeps them as `[the menu](https://example.com/menu)`. Put the cursor in a link and tap **Link** to change its address, or tap **Remove Link** to take the link away. An address that is not a web address shows *That is not a web address. Try again.*

The page works as in Apple Notes: you change the note in place. Tap the words where you want to type. The cursor goes there and the keyboard comes up. There is no **Save** button: the phone keeps each change as you type, and when you leave the page. On a voice note, your words go under the recording's card: the page says *Add notes…* until you type. To fix a word that the phone wrote, tap **Transcript** and type on the recording's page: [Read the transcript](../voice-notes/#read-the-transcript). The recording stays as it was.

To add a recording, tap the **mic** in the bar at the bottom, or in the bar over the keyboard: [Add a recording to a note](../voice-notes/#add-a-recording-to-a-note).

To paste, hold in the words, or tap the blinking cursor. On an iPhone with iOS 16 or later, the iPhone's own menu comes up, with **Paste**, **Copy**, **Cut**, **Select All**, **Look Up** and **Share** as each fits. A second tap on the cursor closes it. **Paste** puts in the words at once, with no *Allow Paste?* question. While you hold and drag the cursor, a magnifier shows the words under your finger. Tap three times on a line to select only that line, not the lines under it.

A paste keeps the formatting that a note can show, as in Notion. Words that you copy from a web page, Notion, Google Docs, Apple Notes, Word or an email come in with their headings, bullet and numbered lists, checklists, quotes, code blocks, and bold, italic, underlined and struck-through words. Colors, fonts, sizes and pictures go: the pasted words take the note's own look. A heading past the third level comes in as a subheading.

- **Markdown becomes formatting.** Words that you copy from ChatGPT, Claude or a text file, such as `## Plan`, `- [ ] Call the bank` or `**now**`, come in as a heading, a checklist item and bold words, not as marks.
- **A link keeps its words.** A link whose words are not its address, such as *the menu*, comes in as those words, linked to the address.
- **A table stays a table.** A table from a web page, Google Docs, Notion or a Markdown table from Claude comes in as a [table](#tables).
- **A list keeps its numbers.** A numbered list with a line under each item, such as *Why: …*, comes in as 1, 2, 3, with each line under its item. A list that has an indent before it, under a label, comes in as a list, not as a sub-list.
- **A few words join the line.** Words with no line style go in at the cursor, and the line keeps its own style.
- **Empty lines stay tidy.** Two or more empty lines in a row become one, and empty lines at the start and the end go.
- **Undo takes back the whole paste** in one step.

A paste works the same with the iPhone's own **Paste**, the app's own menu on Android, a keyboard's ⌘V or Ctrl+V, and the [web page](../on-a-computer/#write-and-change-notes). Words that you copy from inside a note paste back with their styles.

At the top of the page:

- **‹ Notes**, at the left, goes back to the list.
- **Undo**, while you type, after your first change. It takes back your last change, and the keyboard stays up. After an undo, **Undo** and **Redo** show side by side: **Redo** puts the change back. A new change removes **Redo**.
- **Share** and **⋯**, in a round button at the right. **⋯** opens a menu under it with **Pin Note** (or **Unpin Note**), [**Find in Note**](#find-in-a-note), **Copy**, which copies the words without their style marks, and **Delete**.
- **✓**, while you type, closes the keyboard. The change stays.

While you type, a bar floats just above the keyboard, as wide as the screen. It shows **Aa** for [text styles](#text-styles-and-checklists), the checklist button, **Table** to add a [table](#tables), the paperclip to [add photos and files](#add-photos-and-files), and on a phone the **mic** to [add a recording](../voice-notes/#add-a-recording-to-a-note). Swipe the bar to the left for more: bulleted list, numbered list, **Link**, indent less, indent more, and **Hide keyboard**, which closes the keyboard as **✓** does. Part of the next button shows at the edge while more buttons are out of view.

While you select words, the bar changes to the style bar: **B**, **I**, **U**, **S**, **Highlight**, **Link** and **Lists**, which opens bulleted, dashed and numbered lists. A button is lit when the selected words have its style. Tap a lit button to take the style away. When the selection goes, the bar changes back.

With the keyboard closed, a bar at the bottom holds the checklist button, the paperclip, and the round compose button, which starts a new note.

When you type at the end of a long note, the page scrolls so that the line you type stays above the bar.

If you leave a note with no words and no files, the app deletes it for good, as Apple Notes does: [Delete a note](#delete-a-note). A voice note keeps its recording.

## Pin a note

A pinned note stays at the top of your notes list, as in Apple Notes. Pin a note in one of two ways:

- In the notes list, swipe the note's row to the right. The row goes back to its place, and the note moves to **Pinned**.
- On the note's page, tap **⋯**, then **Pin Note**.

The **Pinned** card shows first in the list, above **Today**, with the newest note first. A pinned note shows only there, not in its day group. The card shows only when a note is pinned. [Filters](#filter-your-notes) and [search](#search-your-notes) work in it too: with no pinned match, the card does not show.

Tap **Pinned** to fold the card away. Tap it again to open it. The phone remembers that you folded it.

To unpin a note, swipe its row to the right again, or tap **⋯**, then **Unpin Note**. The note goes back to its day group. With a screen reader, each note's row has a **Pin** or **Unpin** action.

With sending on to Relay Everything, the pin goes to your server, and your other phones and your Relay Everything page show the note under **Pinned** at their next sync. You can pin any note, also one from email or another phone. If two phones change the same note's pin before they sync, the change made later stays. A pin is not a change to the note's words, so your webhooks get no message. With sending off, or with **My own webhook**, the pin stays on this phone.

## Find in a note

Find a word in an open note, as in Apple Notes:

1. On the note's page, tap **⋯**, then **Find in Note**.
2. Type a word in the bar above the keyboard. Upper and lower case are the same. The page selects the first match and scrolls to it, and the bar shows its place, such as *1 of 3*. With no match, it says *No matches*.
3. Tap the down arrow, or return, for the next match, and the up arrow for the one before. After the last match, the first one comes again.
4. Tap **✓** to close the bar. The note's words do not change.

**Find in Note** also finds words in a [table](#tables)'s cells: the match selects the table. **Find in Note** is on the phone only. It shows on a voice note after the phone writes its words. On a computer, use the browser's own find.

## Text styles and checklists

Tap **Aa** while you type. The **Format** panel comes up in place of the keyboard, in groups as in Apple Notes. The style of the line and of the selected words is lit. Tap **✕** to get the keyboard back.

| Style | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title**, **Heading**, **Subheading**, **Body** | Sets the size of the line the cursor is on. Each shows in its own look. The first line of a new note starts as a **Title**. |
| **Monostyled** | Shows the line in a font with letters of one width, as for code. |
| **B**, **I**, **U**, **S** | Makes the words you selected bold, italic, underlined or struck through. |
| **Highlight** | Puts a color behind the words you selected. Tap it again to take the color away. Tap the color dot beside it to pick **Purple**, **Pink**, **Orange**, **Green** or **Blue**. The phone keeps the color you picked for the next time. |
| Bulleted, dashed and numbered lists | Starts a list. Tap return on an empty item to end the list. |
| Block quote | Shows the line as a quote, with a bar at its left. |
| Indent in and out | Moves a line, or a list item, in or out by one step. |

Tap the checklist button to make the line a checklist item, with a round circle at its left. One tap on the circle ticks the item, in green. Another tap clears it. The circle works with the keyboard closed too, so you can tick items as you shop.

You can also type Markdown marks at the start of a line, as in Apple Notes. Type the marks, then a space: the marks go and the line takes the style. Tap **Undo** to get the marks back as words. A paste is not changed in this way.

| Type at the start of a line | The line becomes |
|---|---|
| `# `, `## `, `### ` | A title, a heading or a subheading |
| `* ` or `- ` | A bulleted or a dashed list |
| `1. ` | A numbered list |
| `[] ` | A checklist item |
| `> ` | A block quote |

The page shows each style as it looks. Inside, the note keeps its styles as plain Markdown marks, so your Relay Everything page, your AI and your webhooks read them as normal text:

| On the phone | In the words |
|---|---|
| Title, Heading, Subheading | `# `, `## `, `### ` at the start of the line |
| Monostyled | A code block: a line of three backticks before it and after it |
| Bold, italic, struck through | `**words**`, `_words_` (`*part*` of a word), `~~words~~` |
| Underline | `<u>words</u>` |
| Highlight | `<mark class="pink">words</mark>`, with the color's name |
| A link on words | `[words](https://example.com)`. An address that is its own words stays as it is. |
| A photo, a file or a recording | `![warranty.pdf](attachment:<id>)` on a line of its own, with the file's name and its id |
| A table | A Markdown table: a line of cells between `\|` marks for each row, with a `\| --- \|` line under the first row |
| Bulleted, dashed and numbered lists | `* `, `- ` and `1. ` at the start of the line |
| Checklist | `- [ ] ` open, `- [x] ` ticked |
| Block quote | `> ` at the start of the line |
| Indent | Two spaces for each step in |

Your notes list shows the first line and the start of the rest without these marks. A note that you wrote before styles came opens with the same words, and its first line shows as a title. It does not change until you change it.

## Tables

A note can hold a table, as in Apple Notes.

1. Tap **Table** on the bar over the keyboard. A table of 2 columns and 2 rows comes in at the cursor, with the cursor in its first cell.
2. Type in the cell. The first row shows in bold.
3. Tap return to go to the cell below. Return in the last row adds a row. On a computer, Tab goes to the next cell and Shift+Tab to the one before.

A wide table scrolls to the side. While a cell has the cursor, a handle shows above its column and one at the left of its row:

- The column handle: **Add column before**, **Add column after** and **Delete column**.
- The row handle: **Add row above**, **Add row below** and **Delete row**.

With the cursor in a table, tap **Table** for **Convert to text**, which makes each row a line with a tab between the cells, or **Delete table**. **Undo** takes back each change, also a word typed in a cell. A cell holds plain words: the text styles do not work in a cell.

The note keeps a table as a Markdown table, so your AI and your webhooks read it as one. A table that Claude or another app wrote keeps its own marks, such as `:---:` for a centered column, until you change one of its cells.

## Delete a note

Delete a note in one of two ways:

- In the notes list, swipe the note's row to the left. A swipe to the right [pins the note](#pin-a-note) and deletes nothing.
- On the note's page, tap **⋯**, then **Delete**, the last item, in red. The page closes.

The note leaves the list at once and goes to **Recently Deleted** for 30 days, with its files and its recording. The app shows no message. After 30 days, the note goes for good. With a screen reader, each note's row has a **Delete** action.

To undo the last delete:

- **iPhone:** shake the phone. The app asks **Undo Delete Note**. Tap **Undo**, and the note goes back to its place. A shake while you type undoes your typing, as before.
- **Mac and your Relay Everything page:** press ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) when you are not typing in a note.
- **Android:** there is no shake undo. Recover the note from **Recently Deleted**.

With sending on, the delete goes everywhere: your page and your other phones show the note in **Recently Deleted** at their next sync, and your AI apps no longer find it. Each of your webhooks gets one [`note.deleted`](../../connections/your-database/#notedeleted) message. You can delete any note, also one from email or another phone. A delete made with no signal goes on the next sync. With sending off, the delete stays on this phone until you turn sending on again, so the note does not come back.

If you leave a note with no words and no files, the app deletes it for good, as Apple Notes does: it has nothing to keep, so it does not go to **Recently Deleted**.

### Recently Deleted

While it holds notes, Home shows a **Recently Deleted** row under **Notes**, with its count. Tap it to see the deleted notes, newest delete first.

- **Recover** a note: swipe its row to the left, then tap **Recover**. The note goes back to **Notes**, on every phone and on your page. Each of your webhooks gets one [`note.recovered`](../../connections/your-database/#noterecovered) message.
- **Delete** a note for good: swipe its row to the left, then tap **Delete**. The app asks first, because you can't undo it.
- **⋯** has **Recover All** and **Delete All**. **Delete All** asks first, because you can't undo it.
- Tap a note to read it. You can't change a deleted note: a tap in its words asks you to recover it first.

A note deleted for good goes with its files and its recording, from your server, your page and every phone.

## Change a sent note

With sending on, a change to a note that your server already has goes to your server when you stop typing. Your Relay Everything page and your AI then read the new words in place of the old ones, and each of your webhooks gets one [`note.updated`](../../connections/your-database/#noteupdated) message. With **My own webhook**, the phone sends `note.updated` there itself.

A change made with no signal, or while the note waits to send, goes on the next sync. Only the latest words go. You can change any note on any signed-in phone, also a note from email or another phone: [Your notes on every phone](#your-notes-on-every-phone).

With sending off, a change stays on this phone, as every note does.

## Your notes on every phone

Each phone that you sign in on shows all your notes, as in Apple Notes. It gets the notes from your space: notes from your other phones, your Relay Everything page on a computer, email, Claude Code, Codex, your AI apps and the web API. A new phone gets all your old notes the first time.

New notes and changes come in when you open the app, when you come back to it, when you pull down the list, and every hour in the background. A note from somewhere else shows where it came from, under its title in the list and at the top of the note.

Notes go first. Each time, the phone sends your notes, then gets the new notes. Only then does it send [health](../health/), [places](../location/), the [calendar](../calendar/) and [photos](../photos/). A note that you write while they send goes before the rest. If a health, place, calendar or photo upload fails, your notes still go and come in. That data waits on the phone and goes on a later sync.

- **Edit anywhere.** Change a note on one phone, and your other phones, your page and your AI get the new words at their next sync. Each of your webhooks gets one [`note.updated`](../../connections/your-database/#noteupdated) message.
- **Delete anywhere.** Delete a note on one phone, and it goes to **Recently Deleted** on your other phones at their next sync, and on your page. Your AI no longer finds it: [Delete a note](#delete-a-note). A note open on another phone stays until you leave it, then goes.
- **The newer change wins.** If two phones change the same note before they sync, the change made later stays on every phone and on your page, even when the older change arrives last.
- **Files on tap.** A note from somewhere else lists its files by name and size. Tap one to download it and open it. Only that file downloads, so the phone uses no space or data for files you never open. You add files only to the notes you wrote on this phone.
- **Recordings on tap.** A voice note from another phone shows its transcript at once: tap **Transcript** to read it. Tap **Play** to download its recording and play it. A recording made while sending was off stays only on the phone that made it. An older recorded note's recording downloads and opens on tap, as a file does.
- **Signing out** with **Sign out** on the [Account](../settings/#account) page deletes the notes from somewhere else, and their files, from this phone. The notes that you wrote on this phone stay.

Signed out, the phone gets no notes and makes no network call. Notes it got before stay until you sign out. With **My own webhook**, the phone sends only to your webhook and gets no notes. To get your notes on every phone and keep your own database too, sign in to Relay Everything and add your webhook on your Relay Everything page: your server sends every note and change from every phone to it, and what else you tick for it. Signed in, the phone sends nothing to your webhook itself.

## Search your notes

1. Tap **Search**, at the bottom of Home or of the notes list.
2. Type a word. The list shows only the notes with that word in their words, their recordings' transcripts or their file names. Upper and lower case are the same. On Home, search finds every note. In the notes list, it finds only the notes of the filter you picked.
3. Tap **Cancel** to see Home or the list's notes again.

Search reads only the notes on this phone, so it works with no signal and sends nothing. With no match, the list says *No notes with "<your words>"*.

## The gear and your Relay Everything page

Tap the round gear button at the top right of Home to open [Settings](../settings/).

To open your Relay Everything page, open `<your-relay-server>/` in the phone's browser. Sign in with your email. The notes from all your devices are there. In the app, a tap that changes your account, such as **Delete my account** on the **Account** page, opens this page too. [Account](../settings/#account)

On a computer, open the same address. The page is the same app as on your phone, sized for a computer: you read, write, change and delete your notes there. [On a computer](../on-a-computer/)

## Write a note

1. Tap **New note**, the round green button at the bottom right. A short tap opens a new note. A hold records a voice note: [Talk a note](#talk-a-note).
2. Type the note. The keyboard opens at once. The first line is the note's title.
3. Tap **‹** to go back. The phone keeps the note as you type, so you do not save it.

The note shows at once, with or without a signal. With **Location history** on, the phone adds one point to your location history at the note's time, when it has a fix: [Where you made a note](../location/#where-you-made-a-note). With sending on, the note also goes to your server. If the phone sends a note two times, for example after it lost the server's answer, your server keeps it one time.

## Add photos and files

A note can hold photos, screenshots, PDFs and other files of any kind.

1. Tap **New note**.
2. Type the note, or leave it empty: a note can be only files.
3. Tap **Add photo or file**, the paperclip. A small menu opens beside it: tap **Photos** or **Files**, and pick them from the phone. Each one goes on a line of its own: under the line with the cursor, or at the end of the note when you are not typing.
4. Type under the file, or above it. Words that you type go on their own line, never beside a file.
5. Tap **‹** to go back.

You can add a file to a note later too: open the note and tap the paperclip. With sending on, a file that you add to a sent note goes to your server after the change.

To remove a file, put the cursor on the line under it and tap delete, or select it with words and delete them. Tap **Undo** to get it back while the note is open. When you leave the note, the file goes for good: from this phone and, with sending on, from your server, your page and your other phones. You can remove a file only from a note that you wrote on this phone. On a note from somewhere else, delete does not remove its files.

A note holds up to 10 files, each up to 25 MB. The app refuses a file over 25 MB, or an eleventh file, with a message, and keeps the rest.

In your notes, the first picture shows small at the right of the note's row. On the note's own page, each picture shows full width, and another file shows as a row with its name and size. Tap a file to open it.

Where the files go:

- **On this phone**, always. iPhone backups keep them, because a file you added may be nowhere else. Android cloud backup leaves them out, because it holds at most 25 MB for an app.
- **In your space**, only with sending on. Each file goes after its note, sealed with your space's own key. When you turn sending on, [**Send them**](../../start/connect-a-phone/) sends the files of earlier notes too. [Privacy](../../privacy/)
- **On your Relay Everything page**, a picture shows in its note, and another file shows as a row with its name and size: click it to download it. [On a computer](../on-a-computer/#add-photos-and-files)
- **To your webhooks**, each file after its note, with its bytes: [Your own database](../../connections/your-database/#notefile). A receiver that refuses a file as too large skips it, and the file stays in your space.

[Shortcuts](../shortcuts/) can also add a note with files, with no tap in the app.

To keep a web page, a photo or a file from another app, use its share button: [Share from any app](../share/). A shared note has no place.

## Talk a note

Hold **New note**: an amber circle fills round it in half a second, and the phone buzzes. Until your first voice note, a tip above the button says **Hold to talk**. Let go, and talk: the phone keeps listening on its own full screen, with bars that move with your voice. Tap anywhere on the screen to save, or tap the **X** at the top right to keep nothing. The phone writes the words itself. [Voice notes](../voice-notes/)

## The sync state

The sync state shows only when you can do something about it: when notes wait, or can not go. When all is sent, or sending is off, Home shows no line, also with no signal, and the notes list shows only its count, such as *5 notes*. With a state, the list shows both, such as *5 notes · 2 waiting*. The number counts each waiting note once, with its photos, files and recordings. Health, places and calendar data count on their own folders. The state is words, not a button.

Notes send by themselves: when you make or change a note, when the app opens, and when the phone gets a connection again after it had none. To sync now, pull Home or the notes list down. No button syncs.

| State | Dot | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **2 waiting** | Hollow, or amber and pulsing while they send | Notes made with no signal. They show **Waiting to send** on their rows, and send by themselves when the phone is online again. If the phone is set to reduce motion, the dot stays still. |
| **Key refused** | Red | The server refused this phone's key. [Connect the phone](../../start/connect-a-phone/) with a new key. |
