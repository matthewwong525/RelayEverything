---
title: Settings
description: Each setting in the Relay app, and what it does.
---

Open **Settings** from the gear at the top of your notes. Each switch acts at once, and asks for its permission only when you turn it on.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pin notes to a place** | Each typed note keeps where you were. [Location](../location/#pin-notes-to-a-place) |
| **Location history** | Keeps a point each 100 m you move, so ring notes get a place too. [Location](../location/#location-history) |
| **Health** | Sends steps, heart rate, sleep and workouts every hour. [Health](../health/) |
| **Server** | The server this phone sends to. Change it to try a preview. [Connect a phone](../../start/connect-a-phone/#try-a-preview) |
| **Disconnect this phone** | Removes the key from this phone. Notes not sent yet stay here. You need a new key to connect again. |

The key stays in the iPhone Keychain or the Android Keystore, never in the app's own files.
