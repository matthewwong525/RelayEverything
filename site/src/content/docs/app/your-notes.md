The Relay app opens on your notes: every ring note and typed note, newest first.

## Read your notes

Each note shows:

- **Ring** or **Typed**: where the note came from.
- A microphone mark when the note has a recording.
- The note's words. A ring note with no words shows *No words, only the recording.*
- When you made it, where you were, and which device sent it.

Pull the list down to get new notes. Tap **Show older notes** at the end of the list to read further back.

## Write a note

1. Tap **New note**.
2. Type the note. The keyboard opens at once.
3. Tap **Save**.

The note shows at once. With **Pin notes to a place** on, it keeps where you are; the place follows when the phone has a fix. A note sent two times, for example after the phone lost its answer, is kept once.

## Where a ring note's place comes from

The Pebble ring sends no place. With **Location history** on, a ring note takes the history point nearest in time, up to 10 minutes before or after you spoke. [Location](../location/) tells more.

## The sync mark

The mark at the top says how the phone is with your server. Tap it to sync now.

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | Everything is on your server. |
| **Syncing** | The phone sends and gets notes now. |
| **2 waiting** | Notes made with no signal. They show **Waiting** and go on the next sync. |
| **Offline** | The phone can't reach your server. Nothing is lost. |
| **Key refused** | The server refused this phone's key. [Connect the phone](../../start/connect-a-phone/) with a new key. |
