title: Sign up

You make your Relay Everything account on your server's Relay Everything page. The same steps make a new account or sign in to the one you have. Your account gets its own space, which holds only your data: [Privacy](../../privacy/).

## Make your account

1. Open your server's Relay Everything page, `https://<your-relay-server>/apps/relay/`.
2. Enter your email, then click **Email me a code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the email. It works for 10 minutes, and for 5 tries.

Or click **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**, and sign in there. Relay Everything takes only an email that Apple or Google has checked.

You stay signed in on that browser for 30 days. A new account starts with no notes. The page is the Relay Everything app, sized for your computer: [On a computer](../../app/on-a-computer/).

:::note
On [your own server](../run-your-own/), you sign in with your server's own login, and only your email gets an account. Other people can sign up only when you turn sign-up on.
:::

## Add your first device

1. On the Relay Everything page, click **Settings** at the bottom of the left bar, then **Devices**.
2. Click **Add a device**, and give it a name, such as `Android phone`.
3. The page shows the device's key once, as a QR code. Scan it with the Relay Everything app: [Connect a phone](../connect-a-phone/).

Each phone and each Claude Code computer gets its own key. A key adds data to your space, and a phone's key also syncs your notes: [Keys](../../api/keys/).

## Delete your account

On the Relay Everything page, open **Settings**, then **Account**, and click **Delete my account**. Type your email to confirm. Relay Everything deletes your space, your recordings, your device keys, your sign-ins and your connected AI apps. You can't undo it. The notes on your phone stay on your phone.

## Questions and privacy requests

The shared server's sign-in screen, [app.relayeverything.com](https://app.relayeverything.com), and the **Account** page in Settings on your Relay Everything page show the address to write to with a question or a privacy request, such as a copy of your data. A server that a person runs themselves shows its own address, or none.
