## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Home writes, talks, searches and opens Settings in one tap

Home SHALL have the one *New note* button, which writes on a tap and talks on a hold, and a search field that finds notes in every folder on the phone only. At the top right, Home SHALL have one Settings button that opens Settings in one tap, and no ⋯ menu. On an iPhone, the Settings button SHALL open Settings when the finger lifts, however long the press. A voice note started from Shortcuts SHALL record whatever page is open.

#### Scenario: Settings in one tap

- **WHEN** the person taps the Settings button on Home
- **THEN** Settings opens, with no menu first

#### Scenario: A slow press on the gear

- **WHEN** the person, or the device check on a slow Mac, holds the Settings button on an iPhone for 1 second, then lifts the finger
- **THEN** Settings opens, and no *Settings* name bubble shows
