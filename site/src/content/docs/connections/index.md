Connections move your notes, health and location from Relay to the place you choose, where your own AI can read them. They are coming: none is built yet, so this page gives no steps.

Relay does not store your data for your AI, and it gives you no plugin format or access roles. You keep your own database, tables and tools. Relay collects the data and sends it to them.

## Your own database

:::note[Coming]
Webhooks will send each new note, health reading and place to a database you choose. Its set-up steps come here when it is built.
:::

## AI assistants (MCP)

:::note[Coming]
Your AI assistant will read your Relay data through MCP, the Model Context Protocol. Its set-up steps come here when it is built.
:::
