Why

The app now wears the Relay Everything colors, but each screen still has its old layout: a bare first screen, two floating buttons over the notes, and Settings as one long grey list with lines. This change gives the app the calm, familiar shape of Apple Notes, with one button to write or talk and a way to find a note, while each screen follows the brand's four rules.

What Changes

  1. 1
    A notes list like Apple Notes. A large Notes title with the count and the sync state under it. Notes in groups by day: Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days, Previous 30 days, then by month. Each group is one card with a row per note: the first line in bold, then the time and the rest of the words, then a small Voice or Typed tag, the length, the place and Sent or Waiting to send. A note with a picture shows it small at the right. Tap through every screen, light and dark, below. The Before is today's screen from the last iPhone check. The After is the drawing above, because the phone app does not run in the plan's preview.

  2. 2
    One New note button: tap to write, hold to talk. Search and a round green New note button sit at the bottom, as in Apple Notes. Hold to talk goes away. Tap New note to open a plain page and type. Hold it: after half a second the phone buzzes and the button turns into an amber mic. Let go and the phone keeps listening. Tap anywhere to save the voice note, or tap Cancel. A voice note from Shortcuts or the Action button opens the same card, so you stop it the same way.

  3. 3
    Search your notes. Type in Search and the list shows only the notes with those words, in their words, their place or their file names. It searches this phone only and works with no signal. Cancel shows the whole list again.

Tap a note to read all of it on its own page: the words, pictures, files, and Play for a voice note. You read a note there; you can not edit it.

  5. asked2026-11-07

    Asked, in chat, how holding New note records → after a hold it becomes a mic, the person can let go, and a tap anywhere on the screen ends the recording.

  6. assumed2026-11-07

    Assumed: the hold turns into a mic at 0.5 s, with a small buzz, because the phone's own long press is about that long and the voice notes rule already treats a press under 0.5 s as no recording.

  7. assumed2026-11-07

    Assumed: a tap anywhere saves the voice note and only Cancel throws it away, because slide-off-to-cancel needs a held finger, which the person no longer keeps down.

  8. assumed2026-11-07

    Assumed: the Shortcuts and Action button recording uses the same listening card, because two ways to stop one recording would confuse.

  9. assumed2026-11-07

    Assumed: the large title says Notes and the Signal R sits at the top left, because Apple Notes titles the list by what it holds and the mark keeps the brand on the screen.

  10. assumed2026-11-07

  11. assumed2026-11-07

    Assumed: no pinned notes or folders, because they are new features, not the cleanup asked for.

  12. assumed2026-11-07

  13. assumed2026-11-07

  14. assumed2026-11-07
