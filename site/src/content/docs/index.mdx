## How your data travels

<ol class="relay-flow">
  <li><strong>Collect</strong>Your phone keeps each note. Nothing leaves it until you turn on sending.</li>
  <li><strong>Send</strong>With sending on, the Relay Everything app sends notes, health and location to your own space on a Relay Everything server. Its key only adds data.</li>
  <li><strong>Send on</strong>Webhooks copy each new item to your own database.</li>
  <li><strong>Read with AI</strong>Your AI reads it through MCP. Coming.</li>
</ol>

## A ring to take notes

A ring is one way to take a note: hold the band, talk, and let go. The Relay Everything ring is in design. When it is ready, its notes will come through the phone app, so they will need sending on: see [the ring](ring/how-it-works/).

Your data is in your own space, which only you open. [Sign up](start/sign-up/) on a Relay Everything server, or [run your own in your own Cloudflare account](start/run-your-own/), with one request to your AI, on Cloudflare's free plan.

Relay Everything is built in the open. All of its code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/matthewwong525/RelayEverything), under the MIT license: the phone app, the server, this website, and the ring's models and board.
