Why

The phone app wears Flutter's stock purple-blue, and the website's logo is a ring, which no longer fits: the app comes first, not the ring. Before we clean up the notes screen and the other screens, Relay Everything needs one look to clean them up toward: a logo, colors, type and a few rules that every screen follows.

What Changes

  1. 1
    A brand board you can tap through. It shows five takes on a capital R, each with a relay or a circuit in it: Relay R (the leg is a relay switch), Trace R (routed like a circuit board), Rooted R (three pins join into the stem), Signal R (the bowl sends out waves) and Bolt R (the leg is a bolt of current). Small spark pulses run along the wires. It also shows three color sets (Emerald, a deep green; Signal; Volt), the app icon, the type and the rules for every screen. Tap a logo and a color set: the whole board changes, and your pick comes back with your notes. Tap Dark to see the dark colors.
  2. 2
    The app wears the brand. Clean paper white and ink in place of the purple tint. The main color goes on the one main action, such as New note, with a soft glow. The spark color marks small live moments, such as a voice note or recording. The app gets the Inter font and the logo beside the name at the top. Dark mode gets its own deep colors, where the main color glows. The Android share card uses the same look; the iPhone share sheet keeps the phone's own look for now. The layout of each screen stays as it is: the next plan cleans it up. Section 6 of the board shows the notes screen after. These are today's screens from the last iPhone check. The After is the board's section 6, because the phone app does not run in the plan's preview.

    One brand page that every later change follows. A short page in the wiki says the colors, the type, how to use the logo, and the four rules for every screen.     RuleWhat it means
    One bright thingThe main color marks the one main action on a screen. The spark is for small live moments. Everything else is ink or muted.
    Cards, no bordersA card is a lighter shape on paper. No box inside a box.
    Words with every colorA state says its name: Synced, Waiting, Off. Never color alone.
    Soft corners12 px on buttons and fields, 16 px on cards.

Non-goals: moving or removing anything on a screen (the next plan); the Relay Everything page on your server, which keeps the app's shared look for now; the ring's own pictures and pages on the website; a new name.

  1. Ready Your logo and color pick: You picked Signal R and Emerald on the board.

  3. Ready App Store Connect key for TestFlight builds: in .env and the repository's secrets. The new icon goes out with the next TestFlight build.
