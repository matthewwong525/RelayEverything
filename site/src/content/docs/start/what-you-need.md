You need three things to use Relay: a Relay server, a phone, and a key for each device. A ring to talk into is optional.

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **A Relay server** | It keeps your notes, health and places. It is a private site behind a login. Its owner makes the keys. |
| **An iPhone or an Android phone** | The Relay app shows your notes, and sends a typed note, your health and where you go. |
| **A key for each device** | Each phone and the Pebble app has its own key. You can turn off one key and keep the others. |
| **A Pebble Index ring** (optional) | It sends a note each time you hold it and talk. The Relay ring takes its place when it is ready. |

:::note
The Relay ring is still in design. [The ring](../../ring/how-it-works/) shows how it works and where it is now. Until it is ready, a Pebble Index ring sends the same notes.
:::

## The order

1. [Install the app](../install/) on each phone.
2. [Connect each phone](../connect-a-phone/) to your Relay server with its own key.
3. [Set up the Pebble webhook](../pebble-webhook/), so ring notes go to Relay.
