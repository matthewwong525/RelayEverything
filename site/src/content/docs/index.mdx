## How your data travels

<ol class="relay-flow">
  <li><strong>Collect</strong>Your phone keeps each note. Nothing leaves it until you turn on sending.</li>
  <li><strong>Send</strong>With sending on, the Relay app sends notes, health and location to your Relay server.</li>
  <li><strong>Send on</strong>Webhooks copy each new item to your own database. Coming.</li>
  <li><strong>Read with AI</strong>Your AI reads it through MCP. Coming.</li>
</ol>

## A ring to take notes

A ring is one way to take a note: hold the band, talk, and let go. A Pebble Index ring works today: its notes come through the Pebble app to your Relay server, so they need sending on. The Relay ring is in design: see [the ring](ring/how-it-works/).

Relay is built in the open. The phone app's code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/matthewwong525/relay-app).
