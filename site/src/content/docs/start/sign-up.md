title: Sign up

---

You make your Relay Everything account on your server's Relay Everything page. The same steps make a new account or sign in to the one you have. Your account gets its own space, which holds only your data: [Privacy](../../privacy/).

## Make your account

1. Open your server's Relay Everything page, `https://<your-relay-server>/apps/relay/`.
2. Enter your email, then click **Email me a code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the email. It works for 10 minutes, and for 5 tries.

Or click **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, and sign in there. Relay Everything takes only an email that Apple or Google has checked.

You stay signed in on that browser for 30 days. A new account starts with an empty timeline.

:::note
On [your own server](../run-your-own/), you sign in with your server's own login, and only your email gets an account. Other people can sign up only when you turn sign-up on.
:::

## Add your first device

1. On the Relay Everything page, click **Add a device**.
2. Give it a name, such as `Android phone`.
3. The page shows the device's key once, as a QR code. Scan it with the Relay Everything app: [Connect a phone](../connect-a-phone/).

Each phone and each Claude Code computer gets its own key. A key only adds data to your space: [Keys](../../api/keys/).

## Delete your account

At the bottom of the Relay Everything page, click **Delete my account**, and type your email to confirm. Relay Everything deletes your space, your recordings, your device keys, your sign-ins and your connected AI apps. You can't undo it. The notes on your phone stay on your phone.
