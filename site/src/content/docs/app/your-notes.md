title: Notes
description: Read your ring notes and typed notes, and write a new note on your phone.

The Relay app opens on your notes, newest first. It keeps every note you type on your phone. With sending on, it also shows ring notes from your Relay server.

## Read your notes

Each note shows:

- **Ring** or **Typed**: where the note came from. Ring notes need sending on.
- A microphone mark when the note has a recording.
- The note's words. A ring note with no words shows *No words, only the recording.*
- When you made it, where you were, and which device sent it. With sending off, a pinned note shows **Pinned**: the phone does not look up the place's name.

With sending on, pull the list down to get new notes, and tap **Show older notes** at the end of the list to read further back.

## Write a note

1. Tap **New note**.
2. Type the note. The keyboard opens at once.
3. Tap **Save**.

The note shows at once, with or without a signal. With **Pin notes to where I am** on, it keeps where you are; the pin follows when the phone has a fix. With sending on, the note also goes to your server. A note sent two times, for example after the phone lost its answer, is kept once.

## Where a ring note's place comes from

The Pebble ring sends no place. With sending and **Location history** on, a ring note takes the history point nearest in time, up to 10 minutes before or after you spoke. [Location](../location/) tells more.

## The sync mark

The mark at the top says where your notes are. With sending on, tap it to sync now.

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **On this phone** | Sending is off. Your notes stay on this phone, and the app sends nothing. [Connect a phone](../../start/connect-a-phone/) to turn it on. |
| **Synced** | Everything is on your server. |
| **Syncing** | The phone sends and gets notes now. |
| **2 waiting** | Notes made with no signal. They show **Waiting** and go on the next sync. |
| **Offline** | The phone can't reach your server. Nothing is lost. |
| **Key refused** | The server refused this phone's key. [Connect the phone](../../start/connect-a-phone/) with a new key. |
