Set up the Pebble webhook

Send each note from your Pebble Index ring to your Relay server, with its words and recording.

You need a Relay server and a key for the Pebble app first. Connect a phone shows how to make a key.

Steps

  1. Open the Pebble app, then Index → Webhook.
  2. Set the address to your server's Pebble route:
    https://<your-relay-server>/apps/relay/api/pebble
  3. Add two custom headers with the Pebble app's key: CF-Access-Client-Id and CF-Access-Client-Secret.
  4. Turn on Signing and paste the signing secret.
  5. Tick Transcription and Recording.
  6. Tap Send test. Relay answers 200 and keeps nothing from a test.

What is sent

FieldWhat it holds
audioThe recording, m4a, 16 kHz
transcriptionThe words
recordedAtWhen you spoke, in milliseconds
X-Index-DeliveryOne id per note. A note sent twice is kept once.

If a note doesn't arrive

A 401 means the signature or the key is wrong. Pebble's app tries again for 10 minutes after a 5xx.
